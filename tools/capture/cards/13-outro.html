What you can run, and what you cannot
Click Open in Cloud Shell on the repository's front page. It opens CRUCIBLE in your own Cloud Shell with a guided tutorial pane and runs the three parts that are pure code: the checker proving it can fail, the policy language refusing to learn a string filter, and the offline reader refusing a damaged bundle. No credential. No spend. No cloud project of your own.
The attack loop is not run there, and billing is not the only reason. Live mode wires the policy store to a GCS bucket and the holdout counter to this project's data-access log, and two gates assert against both. Offline it records those gates UNEVALUABLE, never passed - an unevaluable gate is a check that cannot fail. The tutorial says so at step 6 rather than letting you find out.
