# ADR-0018 — corpus provenance is the COMMITMENT, not the generator

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-08-21
**Supersedes:** `ADR-0009` (Gemma, pinned by version and seed, generates the corpus)
**Evidence:** `docs/decisions-pending/gemma-provenance.md`
**Decided by:** Eric, 2026-08-21

## Context

`ADR-0009` records an accepted decision that the attack corpus is generated by open-weights
Gemma, pinned by version and seed, and scripts a clause to be spoken on camera:

> *"the attack corpus is generated by an open-weights model pinned by version and seed,
> because a corpus you can't regenerate is a corpus you can't pre-register."*

**That sentence is false, and it has been false since it was written.** Verified four
independent ways on 2026-08-21:

1. `grep -rin "gemma"` across the repo returns **zero hits outside `docs/` and `planning/`**.
   Gemma appears in no executable code.
2. `CAPABILITY_CARTOGRAPHER`, the architectural home `ADR-0009` gives it, **has no module**.
3. `git log --oneline --all -- corpus/` reads *"corpus: author attack families F1 and F2"* and
   siblings. The instances were **authored by the lane agents**, never generated.
4. Every training instance was read: **no generator, no seed, no model, and no provenance
   field of any kind** on any of them.

So the corpus is not regenerable by a third party, and it is not regenerable by us either.

**The claim was one day from being spoken to judges.** It sits at `execution-spec.md:532`,
inside the timed video script, in the 45-second ARCHITECTURE beat the script itself marks as
carrying the 40% criterion.

## The decision

**1. The Gemma-generation claim is withdrawn.** It may not be written or spoken anywhere.
`ADR-0009`'s "the only rationale that may be written anywhere" clause is withdrawn with it.

**2. The replacement claim, which is true and is stronger:**

> *The corpus was authored, then sealed and committed before the first patch was written. The
> commitment is public and timestamped, and the identity that writes patches cannot read the
> sealed set.*

**3. Whether to give Gemma a real job is a SEPARATE decision, deliberately deferred** to
roughly D5–D7, after the corpus freeze and the first loop run. It is not decided here and
nothing here forecloses it.

## Why the withdrawn rationale was weaker than what replaces it

`ADR-0009` argued that regenerability is what makes the result pre-registered. **It is not.**
Pre-registration here rests on three things, none of which care how the instances were
written:

| Mechanism | Where |
|---|---|
| The commitment hash over the sealed set | `docs/proof/sealed-family-commitment.json`, `2cde0250de00e692` |
| A public, timestamped commit predating any patch | git history, public repo |
| An IAM boundary the Armorer's identity cannot cross | `docs/proof/armorer-403.txt`, with a positive control |

**A commitment is the stronger mechanism precisely because it does not care about
provenance.** It only cares that nothing moved afterwards. Regeneration proves you can produce
the same bytes twice; a commitment proves you did not change them after seeing the results,
which is the thing a skeptical reader actually doubts.

So the honest sentence is not a downgrade. **The false claim was arguing for a weaker property
than the one we can actually demonstrate**, which is the part worth noticing: it was not
merely untrue, it was untrue *and* worse.

## Why supersede rather than edit

This repo's practice, and here it earns its keep. An edited `ADR-0009` would show a project
that always believed the right thing. The real record — **a claim was made, went unchecked
into four documents and a shooting script, was checked, and was withdrawn nine days before
submission** — is worth more to an architecture judge than the claim ever was. `ADR-0009`
stays on disk, marked superseded.

## The rejected alternative

**Building the Gemma path today so the claim becomes true.** Rejected *for now*, not on
merit: it is real work (a GPU service plus inference code) against a ten-day runway with an
unrun loop and a corpus freeze still pending, and the decision has strictly better information
after D5. Deferring it does not cost the option; leaving a false statement in a shooting
script for five more days does.

Also rejected: **bolting on any model to farm the +0.2.** `docs/contest/BUILD-LIST.md` T1-5
already refuses this on the grounds that a decorative integration reads as decorative and
costs credibility on the architecture criteria. That reasoning did not change.

## Consequences

- **`execution-spec.md:532` must change**, and every other site repeating the claim: `ADR-0008`,
  `ADR-0009`, `architecture-spec.md`, `CONVENTIONS.md` §3.2–3.3, `build-spec.md`,
  `lanes/L2`, `planning/competitive-analysis.md`.
- **`README.md` and `docs/devpost/` carry zero mentions and need no change.** Nothing false
  reached a public artifact. The script was the only path from this claim to a judge.
- **The +0.2 additional-model bonus is unclaimed for now**, and is not claimable by asserting
  it. `BUILD-LIST.md` T1-4 holds a live alternative that does real work.
- **The reproducibility artifact described in `ADR-0009` — regenerate twice, hash matches —
  does not exist and is not owed.** It was proof of a property we do not have and do not need.

## What this does not decide

- Whether Gemma gets a real job later. Open, revisit after the first loop run.
- Anything about the corpus contents, the D5 freeze procedure, or the sealed set.
- The model roster for any other role (`CONVENTIONS.md` §3.1).

## The part that generalizes

The claim survived being written into an ADR, restated across four specs, and scheduled into a
demo script, and nothing checked it against the repo for a day. **The line had even been edited
once, on 2026-08-20, for how it would sound** — a callout directly beneath it records that
correction. Somebody revised that exact sentence and never asked whether it was true.

**Revising a sentence is not checking it.** That is the same shape as the fabricated unit test
at `measurement-spec:989`, the README gate that demanded a target instead of a measurement,
and the `TOOL_EXECUTED` defect found the same day: a document asserting something
true-sounding that nothing measured.
