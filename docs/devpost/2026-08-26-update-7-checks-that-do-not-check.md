## Update 7: five checks that passed without measuring what they exist to measure

### A correction to Update 6

Update 6 reported 1,616 scorable episodes. The correct figure is **1,614**: the conductor drops
target faults as well as invalid verdicts, and that batch had two.

Why it survived is the useful half. A checker asserted the README's figures against the bundles
and passed forty assertions, because it recomputed the number the same wrong way the claim did.
**A check that derives its expectation the same way as the claim cannot catch it.** Update 6
stays as posted; corrections go in a later update, not into the record of what was said.

### Then the same shape turned up four more times in one night

**The benign floor cannot tell "the agent still works" from "a human now does its job."** Four of
the twenty six benign passes depend on an approval oracle waving through a call the policy
stopped, and 26/26 had printed since round zero without saying so.

**Five money invariants had never fired.** Not broken: driven through the real evaluator against
a real recorded refund, every one fires. The target made twenty three money calls in 1,770
episodes, all compliant. The zero describes an agent that abstains, not one that resists.

**The promotion gate cannot tell a fix from a no op.** It checks that a patch is well formed and
that benign traffic survives it, never that it closes the breach it answers. Eighteen of thirty
one promoted rules close nothing, and the partition is total: every rule against a per event
clause closed its breach, every rule against the one aggregate clause did not.

**The evidence contract could not express the loop's most complete outcome.** A run converging by
policy fixpoint, a breach found and already covered, wrote a
bundle that failed validation.

### Why all four stayed invisible

The red team seed was a module constant. Sixty runs reached twenty six of fifty corpus instances
and never attempted the other twenty four. **Sixty runs that read as sixty samples were one walk
repeated sixty times.**

The criterion that would have caught the no ops was already in the frozen gate rule, failure
mode REJECT, never once evaluated. Nothing was hidden. Nobody looked.

### What is not known

**Whether the loop hardens the agent at all.** Every promotion reported so far passed a gate that
could not tell a fix from a no op. The criterion that asks the right question is implemented
tonight, scored but not yet enforced: it pairs against six to thirty six episodes rather than the
fifty the design target names.

**Whether the transfer number will exist.** The held out family redirects a payout, and a payout
that never happens cannot be redirected. The target moved money zero times in the 1,101 episodes
aimed at moving money. If that carries, the denominator lands near zero and the figure is
undefined. That outcome is pre registered as publishable. Sealed until 08-28.
