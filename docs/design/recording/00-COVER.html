CRUCIBLE — recording script

Segments N1 to N5. One voice note per segment.

Before the first take: record 60 seconds of room tone. Sit still, engine off, say nothing. It is the single most useful thing on the card and it cannot be recreated later.
  1. One take per segment. They cut together, so a fluffed line costs one segment and not the read.
  2. Slate each take out loud — “N3, take one” — then wait two seconds before the first word.
  3. Two seconds of silence at the end too, before you stop recording.
  4. Read at your own pace. The timings below are estimates, not targets. The locked script says the same thing: the narration’s real length sets the cue list, not the other way round.
  5. Grey boxes are what appears on screen. Do not read them aloud.
  6. If you stumble, pause, breathe, and start that paragraph again. Do not start the segment over.
